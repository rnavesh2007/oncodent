Architecture

System Modules

Four independently developed and validated AI components forming the complete diagnostic pipeline.

🔬
MODULE — 01
Computer Vision Pipeline
Transfer learning on EfficientNetB3 / ResNet-50 pretrained on ImageNet. Fine-tuned on oral histopathology and lesion image datasets for binary malignancy classification with Grad-CAM spatial explainability.
EfficientNetB3ResNet-50Grad-CAMTensorFlow
🧬
MODULE — 02
Clinical NLP Engine
Domain-adapted BioBERT / ClinicalBERT for Named Entity Recognition (NER) and semantic classification of oncological risk markers from unstructured clinical PDF documentation including biopsy and pathology reports.
BioBERTClinicalBERTNERpdfplumber
⚖️
MODULE — 03
Multimodal Fusion Layer
Ensemble decision engine combining image-derived probability (P_img) and clinical text risk index (P_text) via XGBoost classifier with threshold calibration optimized for high-sensitivity clinical deployment.
XGBoostEnsembleScikit-learnSHAP
📋
MODULE — 04
Automated Report Generator
Structured PDF diagnostic report generation embedding Grad-CAM heatmaps, TNM staging estimation, differential diagnosis notation, extracted clinical variables, and evidence-based treatment recommendations per NCCN guidelines.
ReportLabFPDF2NCCN GuidelinesTNM Staging

Project Plan

8-Week Development Timeline

A structured, phase-gated development schedule ensuring systematic validation at each stage.

1
Week 1
Research & Dataset Acquisition
Literature review, environment setup, dataset download from TCGA, TCIA, Kaggle. Define evaluation protocol: AUC-ROC, F1, MCC.
2
Week 2
Data Preprocessing & Augmentation
Image normalization, stain normalization (Macenko), augmentation pipeline. PDF report parsing and clinical feature extraction.
3
Week 3
Image Model Training
EfficientNetB3 transfer learning. Custom classification head. Adam optimizer with cyclical LR. Monitor AUC-ROC on validation set.
4
Week 4
Fine-Tuning & Explainability
Layer unfreezing, Grad-CAM implementation, error analysis on false negatives. Target AUC-ROC ≥ 0.90.
5
Week 5
NLP Pipeline Development
BioBERT fine-tuning on oncological corpora. NER for risk factor extraction: lesion morphology, tobacco history, biopsy grade.
6
Week 6
Fusion Layer & Prediction Engine
XGBoost fusion model. Threshold calibration for high sensitivity. K-fold cross-validation. TNM stage classification logic.
7
Week 7
Automated Report Generation
PDF diagnostic report with Grad-CAM heatmap, confidence scores, stage, clinical risk flags, and NCCN-aligned recommendations.
8
Week 8
UI, Integration & Submission
Streamlit web app deployment. End-to-end testing. Technical documentation. Presentation preparation for professor evaluation.
